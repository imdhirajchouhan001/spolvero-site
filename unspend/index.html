Questions

Something else? Send us a message.

Does Unspend connect to my bank?

No. Unspend never asks for bank details, card numbers or banking logins, and it can't move money. You add subscriptions yourself, which takes about 20 seconds each.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and start adding subscriptions. Signing in with your email is optional, and needed for Pro.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, in storage only Unspend can read. Your subscriptions are never uploaded to us. If you sign in, your email address and name are stored with our sign-in provider, plus your household membership if you share with family.

Will Unspend cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Unspend tracks and reminds. It never cancels or pays anything on your behalf. When a reminder arrives, you decide what to keep.

Which billing cycles are supported?

Monthly and yearly.

Does it convert between currencies?

Not yet. Each subscription keeps the currency it's billed in, and totals add the amounts as entered. For the clearest total, track everything in one currency.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Go to Settings and export a backup file, then restore it on your new phone. The file is yours. Unspend never keeps a copy.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's planned. Join the launch list to hear when it's out.

How do I delete my account?

From inside the app, or email spolvero.design@gmail.com from the address on your account. Account data is removed within 30 days.
